# Bonds Bank Account Features v2.5

This release upgrades each Bonds Bank account type with a modern digital-banking feature set informed by publicly documented capabilities of major U.S. banks.

## Personal checking
Direct deposit, bill pay, mobile deposit where supported, debit/contactless payments, digital wallets, alerts, overdraft preferences, recurring payments, budgeting and external transfers.

## Personal savings
Goals, automatic savings, scheduled transfers, interest/APY display, alerts, statements and linked checking transfers.

## Business
Business debit, POS/payment acceptance, invoicing, payment links, employee cards, role-based access, dual approvals, transaction limits, ACH/wire controls, accounting integrations, payroll/vendor payments, cash-flow tools, fraud controls and deposit tools.

## Business savings
Linked reserve management, automated transfers, interest/APY display, goals, alerts and business-user permissions.

## Consumer credit
Credit limit/available credit, APR and payment information, autopay, alerts, virtual card, lock/replace, disputes, digital wallets and credit-account security controls.

## Business credit
All core credit controls plus employee cards, spend/category controls, receipt capture, expense management, approvals, accounting exports and business credit reporting where supported.

## IRA
Contribution tracking, contribution-year selection, beneficiary management, tax documents, rollovers/transfers, distribution workflow, RMD tracking when applicable and custodian-supported investment/cash options.

## Ledger
Internal accounting controls for cash deposits, reconciliation, double-entry posting, distribution, approvals, reversals, audit records and period close.

## Vault
Restricted reserve/custody controls, dual control, reconciliation, reserve/custody evidence, approval workflow and immutable audit records.

### Card-policy note
Although earlier project requirements requested an autogenerated virtual card for every account type, modern banking design should not automatically attach spending cards to internal Ledger/Vault accounts or IRA assets. Those cards should remain disabled unless an authorized issuer/custodian and the product's governing rules expressly allow them.
