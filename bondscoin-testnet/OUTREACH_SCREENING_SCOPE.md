# Bonds Coin Outreach Screening Scope

> **Status: research scope only.** The list below defines categories to investigate using public sources. It is not a provider list, recommendation, outreach authorization, due-diligence conclusion, or retention decision.

## Screening inputs

The non-binding research list is limited to the following seven prospective-provider categories:

| ID | Category | Evidence to seek from public sources | Explicit exclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| O-01 | Financial-services and digital-asset regulatory counsel | Relevant practice description, target jurisdictions, professional credentials, conflict-check process | Legal opinion, legal advice, or provider retention |
| O-02 | Reserve, treasury, and qualified custody | Regulatory status where applicable, custody/segregation controls, reconciliation/audit artifacts, incident posture | Opening accounts, moving assets, or evaluating liquidity suitability |
| O-03 | AML/CFT, sanctions, and blockchain-risk controls | Product/control scope, sanctioned-address or transaction-risk capabilities, privacy/data controls, independent evidence | Screening an individual, entity, wallet, or transaction |
| O-04 | IRA custodian and tax operations | Self-directed retirement administration capability, jurisdictional availability, reporting/rollover/distribution operations | Characterizing any product as IRS-approved or opening an IRA account |
| O-05 | Independent financial assurance | Public independence/quality-control evidence, relevant financial-controls methodology, secure engagement capability | Providing or commissioning an audit or attestation |
| O-06 | Independent protocol and security assessment | Demonstrated cryptography, protocol, P2P, wallet/custody, cloud, and incident-response assessment capabilities | Sharing non-public code, findings, keys, or credentials |
| O-07 | Launch-governance facilitation | Board/governance and operational-resilience methodology, conflict controls, decision-recording capability | Delegating launch authority or approving mainnet activation |

## Disclosure boundary

Research may use only publicly accessible information. The resulting matrix may include public organization names, URLs, public certifications or registrations, stated services, dated public announcements, and public contact channels. It must not include non-public personal data, private correspondence, security-sensitive implementation details, customer or wallet data, reserve/banking information, credentials, or any direct outreach.

## Screening rule

A prospective provider may appear in the review matrix only as a candidate requiring verification. Public claims are not treated as proof of authority, independence, suitability, availability, price, or regulatory approval. Any recipient-level outreach requires a separate user approval identifying the proposed organization, approved RFI text, and permitted disclosure set.
